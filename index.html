Privacy Policy

Commando Block · Effective date: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android game Commando Block (package name com.commando.blockpuzzle, “the app”) handles information. The app is published by Goo Studio (“we”, “us”, “our”).

The short version

The app does not collect, send, sell, or share any personal information. It works completely offline, has no user accounts, and shows no ads.

Information we collect

None. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send data to us or to anyone else. We do not use analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking tools of any kind.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves the following only on your own device:

  • your best score and Daily Challenge progress (completed days and streak)
  • your coin balance, unlocked skins, and the skin you selected
  • your Adventure mode progress (unlocked levels and stars)
  • your sound setting

This data never leaves your device through the app. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. If you have Android’s backup feature turned on, Android may back up this app data to your own Google account; that is controlled by Google and your device settings, not by us.

Permissions

The app uses only the Vibrate permission, to give haptic feedback when you clear lines. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Third parties

The app does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or social-media code, and we do not share information with third parties. If this ever changes, we will update this policy before releasing the change.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it also collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available at this address, and the effective date above shows when it was last changed.
