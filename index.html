Privacy Policy

Commando Block · Effective date: November 5, 2026 · Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Goo Studio Official (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles information when you use the Commando Block Android game (the “App”).

In short: the App works offline, does not require an account, does not collect your personal information, and does not show ads or use analytics. Your game data stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, receive or store any personal information on our servers. The App does not ask you to create an account or sign in.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

To let you continue playing, the App saves the following locally on your phone:

  • Best scores, coins and unlocked or selected skins
  • Daily Challenge progress and streaks
  • Adventure mode progress and stars
  • Settings such as sound, vibration and whether you have seen the tutorial

This data stays on your device. You can erase it with Reset Progress in Settings, by clearing the App’s storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the App.

3. Permissions

  • Vibrate: used only for haptic feedback during gameplay. You can turn it off in the App’s settings.

The App does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

4. Ads, Analytics and Third-Party Services

The App does not include advertising or analytics services, and does not share data with third parties.

5. Device Backup

Android may back up the App’s data to your Google account if you have device backup turned on. This is controlled by your device and Google account settings and is governed by Google’s privacy policy. We do not receive or access these backups.

6. Links Outside the App

The Settings screen includes links to this Privacy Policy and to the App’s page on Google Play. These open in your browser or the Google Play app, which are governed by their own privacy policies.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you have any concerns, please contact us below.

8. Data Retention and Your Choices

Because your data is stored only on your device, you control it. You can delete it at any time as described in Section 2.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the App safe, but no app is completely secure. Please keep your device updated.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with a new “Last updated” date. If we add features such as ads or analytics in the future, we will update this policy first.
