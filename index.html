Privacy Policy for Commando Block

Effective date: October 31, 2026

App name: Commando Block  |  Platform: Android

Commando Block is an offline arcade block puzzle game. We do not collect, share, or sell your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal details.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

To make the game work, the app saves some data locally on your device only, such as:

  • High scores and game progress
  • Adventure level progress and unlocked skins
  • Daily challenge progress
  • Tutorial completion status
  • Your settings (sound on/off, vibration on/off)

This data never leaves your device through the app, and we have no access to it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app’s storage in your Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

3. Internet Access and Third-Party Services

The app does not request internet permission and works fully offline. The app does not include:

  • Advertising networks
  • Analytics or tracking tools
  • Crash-reporting services
  • Social media SDKs
  • In-app purchases or payment processing

Because of this, no data is sent to us or to any third party.

4. Permissions

The app uses only the following Android permission:

PermissionWhy it is used
VIBRATEProvides haptic feedback (vibration) during gameplay. You can turn this off in the app’s Settings.

5. Device Backup

Android may automatically back up app data (such as your scores and settings) to your own Google account if you have Android backup enabled on your device. This is controlled by your device settings and Google’s services, not by us. We do not receive or access these backups.

6. Children’s Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Since no data is collected, the app is suitable for all ages.

7. Data Security

Because all game data stays on your device, its security depends on your device’s own protections, such as a screen lock. We do not transmit or store your data on any server.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
